What this prototype does

  • Compares manually entered odds for BetWinner, Betika, SportPesa and Odibets.
  • Finds the best odd per outcome and calculates balanced stakes.
  • Runs in a phone browser; it does not place bets or connect to accounts.
  • Live data requires a suitable authorized data feed.
